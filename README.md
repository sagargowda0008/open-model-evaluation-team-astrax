# Open Model Evaluation: Team AstraX

A structured comparison of five open-weight AI models (GPT-OSS-20B, GPT-OSS-120B, Qwen 3.8 27B, Gemma 4 31B, Gemma 4 26B A4B) across reasoning, factual accuracy, instruction following, Indian languages, and safety.

## Contents
- `open_model_evaluation_scorecard.xlsx`: model fact sheet, test prompts, scoring rubric, and results summary
- `Open_Model_Evaluation_Team_AstraX_v3.pptx`: presentation with results and recommendations

## Method
- Models were tested in the Groq Playground and Google AI Studio.
- Every model received the same prompts and settings, with a new conversation for each prompt.
- Two team members scored each response independently.
